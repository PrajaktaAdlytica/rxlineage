The job it owns

Keep the record open to inspection.

The proposed audit model links the episode to source versions, a pharmacist disposition and any later correction. A reversal adds an event; it does not erase the original.

Original offerreconciliation completion and override audit reports.

01

No real source feed or system connection is present.

Workflow

Evidence first. A human at every boundary.

  1. 01

    Preserve the original evidence references.

  2. 02

    Attribute each manual event and timestamp.

  3. 03

    Append corrections while retaining prior history.

Proposed output: Reconciliation completion and override audit reports, with illustrative fixture counts.

Inspect the concept

A history that retains its history.

Completed means an illustrative review state. It does not mean the history is complete, clinically safe or a medication order changed.

Primary user: a reviewing pharmacist and informatics champion. Decision buyer: a health-system pharmacy director or medication-safety lead.
