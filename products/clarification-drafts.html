The job it owns

Draft the question. Keep it unsent.

A reviewer should be able to see which uncertainty motivated a question and who would need to review it. This concept shows a draft only; messaging rights and workflows are not implemented.

Workflow

Evidence first. A human at every boundary.

  1. 01

    Identify the missing or conflicting field.

  2. 02

    Draft a question with its evidence context.

  3. 03

    Keep the draft unsent for human review.

Proposed output: A clarifying-question draft to prescriber/patient, with no external transmission.
