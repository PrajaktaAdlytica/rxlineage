The job it owns

Put the accounts beside each other.

The proposed comparison retains source IDs, observed dates, received dates and field completeness. A reviewer can inspect why the accounts differ before adjudicating a surfaced flag.

Original offerSource-attributed home-med/history comparison.

Proposed inputs / discovery required

01

Synthetic home-med / reported list

02

Dated history extract, with version

03

Source completeness and authorized fields

No real source feed or system connection is present.

Workflow

Evidence first. A human at every boundary.

  1. 01

    Keep the original source and timestamp.

  2. 02

    Separate absent information from disagreement.

  3. 03

    Open the evidence without losing the row.

Proposed output: An inspectable, unresolved comparison—not a reconciled medication order.

Inspect the concept

A state worth looking at.

Source coverage and confidence are not validated. A newer entry is not automatically true.

Primary user: a reviewing pharmacist and informatics champion. Decision buyer: a health-system pharmacy director or medication-safety lead.
